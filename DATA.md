# Data sources

PakDataKit's datasets live in `data/` and are generated by
`scripts/generate-data.mjs` (except `networks.json`, `area-codes.json` and
`holidays.json`, which are edited directly). The exported `dataVersion` is the
date the data was last checked.

**Data version: 2026-10-14**

Official bodies (PTA, NADRA, SBP, Pakistan Post) publish few machine-readable
lists, so some entries rest on secondary sources. Each section says how sure we
are. To correct an entry, open an issue or pull request with a source.

## Banks (`banks.json`): IBAN bank codes

SBP does not publish a list of IBAN bank codes, and IBAN codes do not always
match SWIFT BIC prefixes (e.g. Silkbank is `SAUD`). Codes are therefore checked
against real IBANs.

- **Confirmed from real IBANs:** most commercial and microfinance banks, from
  SBP's account lists for the 2022 flood relief fund and the 2023 Türkiye–Syria
  earthquake relief fund, plus charity donation pages (TCF, Alkhidmat). This
  includes `KHBL` (Khushhali), `JCMA` and `WMBL` (Mobilink Bank), `TMFB`
  (easypaisa), `FMFB` (HBL Microfinance), `FINC` (ABHI), `UMBL`, `NRSP`,
  `APNA`, `BKCH`, `ICBK`, `CITI`, `SUMB` (Bank Makramah), `SAMB`, `SMES` and
  `SAUD`.
- **Confirmed from published IBANs on smaller sites:** `SADA` (SadaPay), `NAYA`
  (NayaPay).
- **Confirmed from real account IBANs shared by a maintainer:** `RQMI` (Raqami
  Islamic Digital Bank) and `MSHQ` (Mashreq Bank Pakistan), both digital banks.
- **Likely, not confirmed by a real IBAN:** `DEUT` (Deutsche Bank), `SBPP`
  (State Bank of Pakistan).
- **Not included, because no code could be confirmed:** HugoBank (in pilot,
  not yet issuing IBANs), KT Bank (reportedly renamed Buraq Bank), LOLC Microfinance
  (formerly Pak Oman), Zarai Taraqiati Bank and Bank of AJK.
- **Merged or renamed** banks keep their code with the current name, e.g.
  `SAUD` → "Silkbank Limited (merged into United Bank Limited)".

## Mobile networks (`networks.json`)

`03XX` prefixes and the operator each was originally allocated to. PTA does not
publish an allocation table; prefixes are cross-checked with Google
libphonenumber and operator announcements.

- `0329` (Jazz) and `0355` (SCO) included.
- `0370`–`0371` (Zong) included; `0372`–`0379` are **not**, as no allocation or
  use was found.
- `0364` (Instaphone, licence cancelled in 2008) is not included.
- `034x` is labelled Telenor, the original allocation; Telenor Pakistan merged
  into PTML (Ufone) in 2026. With number portability, the current operator can
  differ for any prefix.

## Landline area codes (`area-codes.json`)

From PTCL's national dialling-code list, cross-checked with libphonenumber's
geocoding data. Muzaffarabad, Mirpur and Gilgit codes are left out because only
third-party sources give them. Number patterns in `numbers.ts` come from
libphonenumber's PK metadata (v9.0.41).

## Districts (`districts.json`)

177 districts as of October 2026: Punjab 41, Sindh 30, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 40,
Balochistan 41, Gilgit-Baltistan 14, Azad Jammu and Kashmir 10, Islamabad 1.

- **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** (APP, January 2026: "increased to 40"): Lower and Upper
  South Waziristan (2022), Central Dir and Allai (January 2023), Paharpur
  (November 2025), Upper Swat (January 2026).
- **Balochistan** (Revenue Department notification, 8 July 2026; Dawn,
  ProPakistani): Quetta split into Quetta East and Quetta West; Dera Bugti
  became South Dera Bugti and Upper Dera Bugti became North Dera Bugti; new
  Barshore, Tump and Wadh districts; Hub and Usta Muhammad (2022). Taftan is not
  included: no notification was found and the official count of 41 is reached
  without it.
- **Punjab:** Taunsa, Kot Addu, Murree, Wazirabad and Talagang (2022). A 2026
  notification of four more districts was confirmed fake.
- **Sindh:** proposed Karachi district renames (2024) were not found notified,
  so current names are kept.
- Former names (Quetta, Dera Bugti, Upper Dera Bugti, South Waziristan) still
  resolve in `getDistrictProvince`.

## Cities (`cities.json`)

District headquarters and major towns, plus every city that has a postal code
in `postal-codes.json`, each mapped to its province.

## Postal codes (`postal-codes.json`)

Principal post office codes for major cities (109 entries). Pakistan Post has no
downloadable dataset, only a lookup tool, so coverage is limited.

## Public holidays (`holidays.json`)

Eid ul Fitr, Eid ul Adha, Ashura and Eid Milad un Nabi dates actually observed
in 2020–2026, from Cabinet Division notifications and SBP circulars; each entry
has a `source` URL. Later years are estimated from the tabular Hijri calendar
and marked `estimated: true`. Fixed-date holidays follow the years they applied
in (e.g. Iqbal Day restored in 2022, Youm-e-Takbeer from 2024).

## CNIC

First digit = province of registration, per FBR's tax-ID description filed with
the OECD (1 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, 2 former FATA, 3 Punjab, 4 Sindh, 5
Balochistan, 6 Islamabad, 7 Gilgit-Baltistan). `8` for Azad Jammu and Kashmir
comes from secondary sources only. Last digit odd = male, even = female.
