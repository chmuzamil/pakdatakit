## Districts (`districts.json`)

177 districts as of October 2026: Punjab 41, Sindh 30, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 40,
Balochistan 41, Gilgit-Baltistan 14, Azad Jammu and Kashmir 10, Islamabad 1.

> **Unverified: Central Dir, Allai and Paharpur** (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa). They
> rest on a news report of January 2026, not on a notification. Official
> pages updated in November 2026 do not show them: Malakand Division lists
> 10 districts without Central Dir (cmd.kp.gov.pk), Hazara Division lists 8
> without Allai (chd.kp.gov.pk), and the KP Bureau of Statistics'
> Development Statistics 2026 has Allai as a tehsil of Batagram and Paharpur
> as a tehsil of Dera Ismail Khan (all retrieved 2026-11-16). They are kept
> until a notification settles it either way. Nine more districts are also
> not confirmed on an official page; see the end of this section.

- **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** (APP, January 2026: "increased to 40"): Lower and Upper
  South Waziristan (2022), Central Dir and Allai (January 2023), Paharpur
  (November 2025), Bar Swat (January 2026; reported as "Upper Swat" before
  the Board of Revenue notification of 27 January 2026).
- **Balochistan** (Revenue Department notification, 8 July 2026; Dawn,
  ProPakistani): Quetta split into Quetta East and Quetta West; Dera Bugti
  became South Dera Bugti and Upper Dera Bugti became North Dera Bugti; new
  Barshore, Tump and Wadh districts; Hub and Usta Muhammad (2022). Taftan is not
  included: no notification was found and the official count of 41 is reached
  without it.
- **Punjab:** Taunsa, Kot Addu, Murree, Wazirabad and Talagang (2022). A 2026
  notification of four more districts was confirmed fake.
- **Sindh:** proposed Karachi district renames (2024) were not found notified,
  so current names are kept.
- Former names (Quetta, Dera Bugti, Upper Dera Bugti, South Waziristan, Upper
  Swat) still resolve in `getDistrictProvince`.
- **Official confirmation, checked 2026-11-16** (details in AUDIT.md,
  section 9). Confirmed on official pages: Murree, Talagang, Wazirabad, Kot
  Addu and Taunsa (Punjab Board of Revenue list); Lower and Upper South
  Waziristan and Hub (Election Commission delimitation report, 2023); Bar
  Swat (Malakand Division's website); Usta Muhammad, Barshore and Tump
  (Balochistan budget documents, June 2026). **Not confirmed on any official
  page that could be read:** Central Dir, Allai and Paharpur (Khyber
  Pakhtunkhwa's divisional websites and its Development Statistics 2026
  still show Allai and Paharpur as tehsils and list no Central Dir); Quetta
  East and West, North and South Dera Bugti and Wadh (Balochistan); and
  Darel, Tangir, Gupis-Yasin and Roundu (Gilgit-Baltistan's 2024-25
  development programme treats them as sub-divisions). These stay in the
  dataset on the strength of the notifications and reports cited above, and
  are the entries most likely to need correcting.
