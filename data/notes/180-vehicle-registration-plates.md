## Vehicle registration plates

`parseVehiclePlate` accepts 1–4 letters, then 1–4 digits, optionally followed
by a two-digit year after a separator (`LEA-1234-19`). There is no national
format: each province's excise department sets its own.

- **Reported formats** (Wikipedia, partly inconsistent,
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Pakistan):
  Sindh cars 3 letters + 3 digits; Islamabad and Balochistan 2 letters + 3
  digits; Khyber Pakhtunkhwa 2 letters + 4 digits; Gilgit-Baltistan 3 letters +
  2 digits; AJK motorcycles and public transport 4 letters + 3 digits; older
  Punjab plates 4 digits plus a two-digit year.
- **Number length:** Punjab's excise department auctions registration marks of
  1 to 4 digits (https://excise.punjab.gov.pk/auctions).
- **Recent changes:** numbers now stay with the owner in Islamabad
  (https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2025/07/20/ict-excise-deptt-introduces-personalized-number-plates-digital-cards-online-tax-payment/),
  Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
  (https://arynews.tv/kp-new-vehicle-registration-number-plate-system-sep-17-2025)
  and Sindh, from 16 February 2026
  (https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/02/16/sindh-enforces-cnic-based-number-plates-owners-can-retain-registration-marks/).
  Punjab announced new series from May 2026
  (https://propakistani.pk/2026/04/29/punjab-to-launch-five-new-vehicle-number-series/)
  without publishing their layout.
- **Not checked:** series letters against districts (no official list, e.g. for
  `LEA`/`LEB` = Lahore), and labels printed on the plate such as "ICT" or
  "PUNJAB", which are not part of the number.
