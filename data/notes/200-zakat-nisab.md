## Zakat nisab (`zakat-nisab.json`)

The nisab for compulsory Zakat deduction at source from savings, profit and
loss sharing and similar accounts (asset code 101), for each Zakat year from
1433 AH (2012) to 1447 AH (2026). Each record comes from two documents,
retrieved 2026-11-23:

- the Ministry of Poverty Alleviation & Social Safety's notification of the
  nisab fixed by the Administrator-General Zakat, which states it in
  figures and in words (they agree in every year); and
- the SBP BPRD circular letter that sends it to banks, with SBP's bank
  holiday circular for the deduction day where there is one.

SBP's www site blocks automated downloads, so the files were read from
SBP's own mirror, `archive.sbp.org.pk`, at the same paths. The scanned
notifications (2012-2024) were read from the page images.

**Rules** (Zakat and Ushr Ordinance 1980 and the Zakat (Collection and
Refund) Rules 1981, from pakistancode.gov.pk and pass.gov.pk):

- **Rate:** 2.5% of the balance at the start of the valuation date (First
  Schedule, S. No. 1).
- **Accounts:** the nisab applies to savings and similar accounts (asset
  code 101). Current accounts and foreign currency accounts are not subject
  to compulsory deduction (Second Schedule, S. No. 4).
- **Exemption:** a holder who files the declaration on Form CZ-50 at least
  30 days before the valuation date is not deducted (s. 1(3), proviso (a);
  rule 20).

**Rounding:** Zakat is rounded to the nearest rupee, as the First
Schedule's Note 2 says: an amount under a rupee is not charged; otherwise 50
paisa or more counts as a rupee and less than 50 paisa is dropped.

**Exemption:** the declaration (Form CZ-50) must be filed with the bank at
least 30 days before the valuation date, sworn before a magistrate, oath
commissioner, notary public or other person authorised to administer
oaths, in the presence of two witnesses (s. 1(3), proviso (a)). The library
takes `exempt: true` to mean such a declaration is on file; it does not
check it.

**Conventions and limits:**

- `zakatYear` is the Hijri year of the valuation date, 1 Ramazan (`1447` is
  February 2026); the notification calls the same deduction "1446-47"
  (`label`). The Ordinance says a Zakat year starts on 1 Ramazan, which
  would name it 1447-48.
- **Valuation date (`ramazan1`):** 1 Ramazan as the Central
  Ruet-e-Hilal Committee announced it, with the outlet that reported the
  announcement (`ramazan1Source`); dates are assigned to Zakat years by it.
  The notifications only say 1 Ramazan is "likely to fall on" one of two
  days; that text is kept as a quoted `note`.
- **Deduction day (`bankHolidayDate`):** the bank holiday SBP declares for
  the deduction. It is 1 Ramazan, or the next working day when 1 Ramazan is
  a weekend or public holiday: 1433 (SBP BPRD Circular Letter No. 17 of
  2012: 1 Ramzan on Saturday or Sunday, so Monday 23 July 2012), 1438,
  1441, 1443, 1446, and 1444 (1 Ramazan was Pakistan Day, 23 March 2023, so
  24 March). `null` for 1434 and 1435, where no holiday circular was found.
- **Boundary:** a balance equal to the nisab is deducted. Every
  notification from 1433 to 1447 says no deduction where the balance is
  "less than" the nisab (2026: deduction on "Rs. 503,529/- or above"), and
  s. 2(xxiii) defines a sahib-e-nisab as a person who owns assets "not less
  than nisab". The First Schedule, S. No. 1, as printed by Pakistan Code,
  says no deduction where the balance "does not exceed" the nisab, which
  would not deduct it.
- **Provinces:** in Punjab the Punjab Zakat and Ushr Act 2018 (s. 27(1))
  repealed the 1980 Ordinance; its First Schedule uses the same "does not
  exceed" wording, with the nisab "as notified by the Chief Administrator
  Zakat"
  (https://punjabcode.punjab.gov.pk/uploads/articles/the-punjab-zakat-and-ushr-act-2018-pdf.pdf).
- **Not covered:** years before 1433 AH (no nisab circular in SBP's indexes
  for 2005-2011) and any later amendment to the 1981 Rules or Form CZ-50
  (the Rules were read in their 1989 consolidation).
- Document quirks: the 1433 notification itself reads "No. CE-1001(36)/NISAB/1432-33/2012" (checked on the scanned letter), and SBP's circular quotes it as
  "CE-100(36)"; the 1436 number ends "/2015/" with the serial blank in
  the original, and SBP BPRD Circular Letter No. 14 of 2015 prints it the
  same way; the 1442 notification, dated April 2021, prints the deduction
  month as "April, 2020".
