Sales tax on goods is federal (FBR). Sales tax on services is provincial: PRA in Punjab, SRB in Sindh, KPRA in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, BRA in Balochistan, and the federal ICT tax in Islamabad. Each rate carries the law and source it comes from.

## Reference

### getSalesTaxRate()

```ts
function getSalesTaxRate(query: {
  kind: 'goods' | 'services';
  province?: string; // required for services
  date?: string | Date; // default: today
}): SalesTaxRate | null;
```

**Returns** the standard rate in force on that date, with `authority`, `rate`, `from`, `to`, `law` and `source`.

**Caveats.** Returns `null` for dates before 1 July 2019, unknown provinces, services without a province, or goods in Azad Jammu and Kashmir or Gilgit-Baltistan. Standard rates only: reduced rates, exemptions, the Third Schedule and further tax are not covered.

### calculateSalesTax()

```ts
function calculateSalesTax(
  amount: number,
  options: {
    rate?: number;
    province?: string;
    kind?: 'goods' | 'services';
    date?: string | Date;
    inclusive?: boolean;
  },
): SalesTaxAmounts | null; // { net, tax, gross, rate }
```

**Returns** the net amount, tax and gross amount in whole paisa. With `inclusive: true`, `amount` already includes tax and is split.

### splitInclusive()

```ts
function splitInclusive(gross: number, rate: number): SalesTaxAmounts | null;
```

**Returns** the net and tax parts of a tax-inclusive amount at a given rate.
