On 1 Ramazan, banks deduct Zakat at source from savings, profit and loss sharing and similar accounts (asset code 101) under the Zakat and Ushr Ordinance 1980. Nothing is deducted from a balance below that year's nisab, or when the holder has filed the exemption declaration (Form CZ-50). Each year's nisab comes from the Ministry of Poverty Alleviation & Social Safety's notification and the SBP circular letter that sends it to banks.

This covers deduction at source only. Current accounts and foreign currency accounts are not deducted, and it is not a religious ruling on the Zakat a person owes.

## Reference

### getZakatNisab()

```ts
function getZakatNisab(
  query: { zakatYear: number | string } | { date: string | Date },
): ZakatNisab | null;
// { zakatYear, label, nisab, valuationDateHijri, expectedValuationDates,
//   bankHolidayDate, notification, sbpCircular, source }
```

**Returns** the year's nisab with its notification and SBP circular (number, date, URL). `zakatYear` is the Hijri year of 1 Ramazan (`1447` is February 2026). The notification's own label, such as `'1446-47'`, works too.

**Caveats.** Where 1 Ramazan's date is confirmed (`valuationDate`: 19 February 2026 for 1447), that date starts the year. For other years a notification gives only the two likely days, because the day depends on the moon sighting; for a date on the first of those two days the year is unknown and the result is `null`. Years before 1433 AH and after the last notified year are `null`.

### zakatDeduction()

```ts
function zakatDeduction(input: {
  balance: number;
  zakatYear?: number | string;
  date?: string | Date;
  exempt?: boolean;
}): ZakatDeduction | null;
// { zakatYear, nisab, balance, deductible, reason, rate, zakat, source }
```

**Returns** 2.5% of the whole balance, rounded to the nearest rupee (First Schedule, Note 2), when the balance is at or above the nisab. `reason` is `'below-nisab'` or `'exempt'` when nothing is deducted.

**Caveats.** `exempt: true` means a valid declaration on Form CZ-50 is on file: filed at least 30 days before 1 Ramazan, sworn before an authorised person with two witnesses. The library does not check it. A balance exactly equal to the nisab is deducted, as the notifications say ("Rs. 503,529/- or above" in 2026). The Ordinance's own wording ("does not exceed") would not deduct it.
