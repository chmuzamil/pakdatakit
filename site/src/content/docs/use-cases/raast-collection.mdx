## The problem

A shop, school or freelancer wants customers to pay into a bank account from any banking app, Easypaisa or JazzCash, without typing the IBAN or the amount. A Raast QR carries the IBAN, and optionally the amount and a short note such as the order number. The library builds that QR text in your own code, for each order.

## 1. Check the IBAN you collect into

`validateIBAN()` checks the IBAN's format and checksum, and `getBankFromIBAN()` names its bank. A QR builder throws a `RangeError` for an invalid IBAN, so check it when you save your settings, not when a customer checks out.

<LangTabs module="use-raast-collection" ids={['iban', 'bank']} />

## 2. Build a QR for the order

`createRaastP2PQr()` returns the QR payload text. Without an amount the QR is static and the payer types the amount. With an amount it is dynamic and needs `expiresAt` or `expiresIn`. `particulars` (up to 30 characters) carries a note such as the order number.

<LangTabs module="use-raast-collection" ids={['qr']} />

## 3. Test it before you use it

`parseRaastQr()` reads a payload back: the IBAN, whether its checksum and the QR's CRC are valid, the amount and the expiry. Then scan the QR with your own bank's app before customers do.

## 4. Draw the QR

## Pitfalls

- **An amount without an expiry.** A QR with an amount needs `expiresAt` or `expiresIn`; without one, the builder throws a `RangeError`.
- **Using the scheme in SBP's document.** Person-to-person QR codes use scheme `00`: banking apps reject the `30` in SBP's document. The library uses `00` by default.
- **A long note.** `particulars` is at most 30 characters, and the bank name 25.
- **Skipping the test scan.** Test-scan with your bank's app before using a QR for payments.
- **A merchant QR without your bank's template.** SBP leaves the layout of the Raast merchant account template to the scheme and has shared it only with banks. `createMerchantQr()` needs the template sub-fields your bank gives you.

## What this library does not do

- **It does not tell you whether a customer paid.** A QR carries payment details; it is not a payment. Confirm each payment in your bank account.
- **It does not move money or talk to any bank, Raast or SBP.** It builds and reads QR text, offline.
- **It does not open a merchant account** or give you a merchant template; that comes from your bank.
- **It does not draw the QR image.** Use any QR library, or the `RaastQR` component in the React and Flutter kits.

## Source

The State Bank of Pakistan's QR standard (Circular Letter No. 1 of 2022), as described in the [Raast QR guide](/docs/raast/).
